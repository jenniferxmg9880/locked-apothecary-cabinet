HEMLOCK REDUCTION
Catalogued under: restricted botanicals, second shelf

A student's notation, copied faithfully:

"The stem gives it away before the flower does — spotted, the way
old parchment spots with age, if age were a warning instead of a
color. Reduce it slowly, never over open flame, and stop the moment
the liquid stops moving on its own when the vessel is tilted.

Socrates is the lesson every first-year is told before they're told
anything else about this plant. Ask your proctor why, if you were
somehow not told."

— filed by order of the Herbology-Alchemy joint faculty, unsigned
