WOLFSBANE TINCTURE
Catalogued under: restricted botanicals, second shelf

A student's notation, copied faithfully:

"Gather the deep violet hoods at dusk, when the petals have folded but
not yet closed. Press them, still dew-wet, between two panes of cold
glass until the color bleeds through. Steep the stain in still water
under a moon no more than three nights from new, and do not stir it —
the liquid will darken on its own, from violet to near-black, over
the course of a night.

Do not taste it to test its strength. It has no safe strength to find."

— filed by order of the Herbology-Alchemy joint faculty, unsigned
