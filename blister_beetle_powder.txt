BLISTER BEETLE POWDER
Catalogued under: restricted zoologicals, third shelf

A student's notation, copied faithfully:

"Dried and ground, the beetle looks like nothing more than rust-
colored dust — which is exactly why this shelf is locked and the
last one wasn't. It raises blisters through cloth if you're careless
handling the dry powder, let alone what it does taken any other way.

History remembers this one as a poison mistaken, more than once, for
a cure. That confusion killed people who trusted the mistake."

— filed by order of the Herbology-Alchemy joint faculty, unsigned
