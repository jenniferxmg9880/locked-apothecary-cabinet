OLEANDER DRAUGHT
Catalogued under: restricted botanicals, second shelf

A student's notation, copied faithfully:

"Every part of the shrub is usable and every part is the same
warning repeated. Strip the leaves along their spine, dry them
somewhere the smoke of burning them could never reach a kitchen,
and grind only what a closed fist can hold at once.

The draught wants nothing added to it. Anything you introduce
just changes how quickly it works, never whether it does."

— filed by order of the Herbology-Alchemy joint faculty, unsigned
