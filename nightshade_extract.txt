NIGHTSHADE EXTRACT
Catalogued under: restricted botanicals, second shelf

A student's notation, copied faithfully:

"Black berries, never more than seven, crushed with the flat of a
silver spoon — iron taints the work and cannot be used. Let the pulp
rest in shadow until it weeps its own liquor; do not add water, do
not hurry it. What separates on its own is what you keep.

Old apothecaries called a drop of this 'beautiful' for what it does
to the eyes before it does anything else. That is not a reason to
be curious about the rest."

— filed by order of the Herbology-Alchemy joint faculty, unsigned
